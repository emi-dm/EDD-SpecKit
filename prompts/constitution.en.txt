/speckit-constitution Create the principles for a small
Python command-line project maintained by students.
Priorities: (1) every business rule must be covered by at
least one automated test with an edge case; (2) amount and
date calculations must be deterministic and must not depend
on the machine's time zone; (3) no external dependencies
other than the test framework; (4) any visible behaviour
change requires updating the specification before the code;
(5) the code must be clean and readable.
